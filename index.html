About The Architech Design

We Design More Than Spaces. We Design Experiences.

Since 2015, The Architech Design has been creating beautiful, functional, and inspiring spaces for people who want their surroundings to feel truly their own. We bring together thoughtful architecture, refined interiors, and practical planning to shape spaces that are made for everyday living.

Based in Gorakhpur, we work with families, business owners, and builders across Gorakhpur, Lucknow, Delhi NCR, and nearby cities. Whether you are planning a new home, renovating your interiors, or developing a commercial space, our team helps you move from rough ideas to a practical design, clear budget, and confident execution plan.

Questions

Before You Start

Quick answers for budget, service area, packages, and project planning.

Do you work outside Gorakhpur?

Yes. The studio is based in Gorakhpur and supports projects across Gorakhpur, Lucknow, Delhi NCR, and nearby cities depending on the scope.

How accurate is the budget estimator?

It gives a starting range for early planning. Final pricing depends on measurements, materials, finish level, furniture details, and execution scope.

Can I choose only design, not execution?

Yes. You can discuss design planning, 2D/3D visualization, material guidance, or a more complete design-and-execution support package.

What should I share for a consultation?

Share your location, room sizes or floor plan, photos or videos of the site, budget range, preferred style, and expected timeline.
